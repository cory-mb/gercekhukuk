## Application requirements have changed

The requirements for associate professorship (doçentlik) applications were amended by decision no. 2023.10.183, adopted at the **10th session of the General Assembly of Higher Education on 15 June 2023**.

## Scope of the changes

The new rules update the minimum publication criteria and application conditions in several fields. These changes directly affect both ongoing and future associate professorship processes.

## Who is affected

- Candidates who have not yet applied and will apply under the new criteria
- Candidates with pending applications affected by the change
- Candidates who were previously rejected and are considering reapplying

## Legal assessment

Whether the amended criteria can be applied retroactively, and how they affect pending applications, requires separate legal analysis. Candidates facing problems with a pending application are advised to seek legal advice.
