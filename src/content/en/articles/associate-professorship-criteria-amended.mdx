The requirements for associate professorship (doçentlik) applications were amended by decision no. 2023.10.183, adopted at the 10th session of the General Assembly of Higher Education on 15.06.2023. Following a subsequent review, that decision was itself amended and found appropriate in the form set out in decision no. 2023.12.218, adopted at the 12th session on 17.08.2023.

This amendment will not affect candidates applying in the October 2023 period. Candidates applying for associate professorship in the March 2024 period will be subject to the amended application requirements.

You can access the Inter-University Council Presidency's announcement of 09.08.2023 on the first amendment, together with the new application criteria, at the link below.

[https://www.uak.gov.tr/Sayfalar/Haberler/2023/8/docentlik-basvuru-sartlarinin-degisikligine-iliskin-duyuru.aspx](https://www.uak.gov.tr/Sayfalar/Haberler/2023/8/docentlik-basvuru-sartlarinin-degisikligine-iliskin-duyuru.aspx)

You can access the Inter-University Council Presidency's announcement of 24.08.2023 on the second amendment, together with the amended and approved form of the associate professorship criteria, at the link below.
