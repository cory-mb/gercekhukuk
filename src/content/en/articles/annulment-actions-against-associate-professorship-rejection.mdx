## Who Is an Associate Professor (Doçent)?

The definition of associate professor is given in Article 3 of the Higher Education Law no. 2547, titled "Definitions", in paragraph (2) of sub-paragraph (m), titled "Faculty Members". Accordingly, an Associate Professor is a person who holds the academic title of associate professor (doçentlik) awarded by the Inter-University Council.

## What Is the Inter-University Council (ÜAK)?

Under Articles 3/b and 11 of the Higher Education Law no. 2547, the Inter-University Council is a supreme body composed of university rectors and one professor from each university, elected for four years by that university's senate.

The Inter-University Council is an academic body. Under Article 11/b of Law no. 2547, one of the Council's most important duties is to form juries in the relevant scientific or artistic field for associate professorship applications, to evaluate candidates' publications and work within the principles and procedures determined by the Council of Higher Education, and to award the associate professorship title to candidates with sufficient publications and work.

## What Are the Purpose and Scope of the Associate Professorship Regulation?

The purpose of the Associate Professorship Regulation, which entered into force upon its publication in the Official Gazette no. 30392 dated 15.04.2018, is to determine the procedures and principles for associate professorship evaluation. The Regulation covers the determination of associate professorship scientific/artistic fields, the conditions for applying, the time and procedure of application, the formation of associate professorship evaluation juries, the conduct of the associate professorship process, and the provisions on the formation and duties of the commissions and the procedures to be followed for allegations of plagiarism, other breaches of scientific research and publication ethics, and disciplinary violations determined by the Inter-University Council.

## When Can an Associate Professorship Application Be Made?

Unless another date is determined by the Inter-University Council, associate professorship applications are made twice a year, starting on the twentieth day of March and October and ending at the close of working hours on the last working day of the relevant month at the latest.

## How Is an Associate Professorship Application Made?

Associate professorship applications are made by uploading documents and all academic works to the Inter-University Council electronically. Where the original or a certified copy of documents is required, candidates send these to the Inter-University Council by hand or by post. The Associate Professorship Commission may also decide to have the academic works of candidates applying in the scientific/artistic field it deems necessary, which form the basis of the evaluation, physically sent to the jury members.

## What Are the Associate Professorship Application Criteria?

- Having completed a doctorate in Türkiye, having obtained a specialist title in medicine, dentistry, pharmacy or veterinary medicine, or having gained proficiency in one of the branches of art determined by the Council of Higher Education upon the recommendation of the Inter-University Council, or, where the doctorate, proficiency in art, or specialisation in medicine, dentistry, pharmacy or veterinary medicine was obtained abroad, holding the equivalence of that title.
- Having received at least fifty-five points in a central foreign language examination determined by the Council of Higher Education, or an equivalent score in a foreign language examination whose international validity is accepted by the Council of Higher Education. Where the associate professorship scientific field relates to a particular foreign language, taking this examination in another foreign language.
- Having produced original scientific publications and works of the minimum number and quality to be determined by the Council of Higher Education, upon the opinion of the Inter-University Council, taking into account the characteristics of each scientific or artistic discipline.

## How Are Associate Professorship Applications Evaluated?

Associate professorship applications are evaluated in terms of whether the candidate has acted in accordance with scientific research and publication ethics principles, whether the candidate meets the minimum application requirements, the quality and originality of the works, the candidate's contribution to the scientific field, and the candidate's competence in the field as a whole. A candidate's having met the minimum application requirements does not mean the candidate has succeeded in the evaluation of works. The jury's evaluation reports regarding a breach of scientific research and publication ethics are reviewed by the relevant Scientific Research and Publication Ethics Commission. The jury's evaluation reports stating that the candidate does not meet the minimum application requirements are reviewed by the Associate Professorship Commission.

The associate professorship application of a candidate found not to meet the minimum application requirements is cancelled by the Associate Professorship Commission. The jury report(s) stating that the minimum application requirements are not met are made available electronically to the candidate, with names concealed, together with the Associate Professorship Commission's decision. The reports of the other jury members are not made available to the candidate. If a candidate whose application was cancelled on the ground of not meeting the minimum application requirements reapplies in the same scientific/artistic field, the information and documents of the application period that was the subject of the cancellation, and the Associate Professorship Commission's decision that the minimum application requirements were not met, are made available to the jury members in the new application.

If the candidate is found to meet the minimum application requirements, the Associate Professorship Commission gives the relevant jury member(s) additional time to prepare an evaluation report of "successful/unsuccessful". The duty of a jury member who does not prepare a report within the additional time is terminated by the Associate Professorship Commission. In place of members whose duty has been terminated and members who insist on the opinion that the "minimum application requirements are not met", the reports of substitute members are taken into consideration in order. The associate professorship evaluation jury prepares a detailed and reasoned evaluation report stating whether it finds "successful" or "unsuccessful" a candidate in respect of whom it has found no breach of scientific research and publication ethics and who it has determined meets the minimum application requirements, on the basis of the quality of the candidate's academic work and its contribution to the scientific/artistic field. The jury member uploads the report to the Associate Professorship Information System within the time limit.

When the jury reports are completed, the reports taken as the basis of the evaluation and the result of the application are made available electronically to those concerned, and are deemed notified to them on the fifth day following the date on which they were made available. The reports taken as the basis of the evaluation are: where the application is cancelled for an ethics violation, the jury report(s) that found the ethics violation; where it is cancelled for failure to meet the minimum requirements, the jury report(s) stating that the minimum application requirements are not met; and where the candidate is successful/unsuccessful, the reports prepared by the principal jury members. Reports other than those taken as the basis of the evaluation are not made available to candidates. In juries of five members, a candidate is deemed successful if at least three members find the candidate successful; in juries of three members, if at least two members do so.

If a candidate who applied for associate professorship and was unsuccessful reapplies in the same scientific/artistic field, the information and documents of the last period in which the candidate was unsuccessful, and the reports on which the finding of failure was based, are made available to the jury members in the new application, with the names of the jury members concealed. The candidate who is successful as a result of the evaluation of scientific/artistic publications and academic works is awarded the "Associate Professorship Title" in the relevant scientific/artistic field by decision of the Inter-University Council Executive Board, and an "Associate Professorship Certificate" is issued.

## When Can a Candidate Whose Associate Professorship Application Has Been Rejected Reapply?

The associate professorship application of a candidate found to have missing/erroneous education documents, thesis, foreign language certificate or other documents required for the application, and/or to have applied in a manner contrary to the procedure in the associate professorship application guide, is rejected by the Inter-University Council Presidency. A candidate whose application is rejected on these grounds may reapply for associate professorship in following periods.

A candidate whose application was cancelled on the ground of not meeting the minimum application requirements may reapply in the following period, provided that the candidate meets the application requirements taking the grounds for cancellation into account. Applications made without remedying the deficiencies that were the subject of the cancellation are rejected by the Associate Professorship Commission.

A candidate found unsuccessful by the associate professorship evaluation jury may reapply, counting from the application period, at the earliest in the third following period, provided that the candidate completes the works and activities found deficient by the jury.

A candidate found, as a result of the review of an allegation of breach of scientific research and publication ethics, to have committed an ethics violation, and whose application was cancelled, may reapply, counting from the application period, at the earliest in the third following period.

A candidate whose application was cancelled upon the determination that misleading information or documents had been submitted may reapply, counting from the application period, at the earliest in the third following period.

Candidates whose applications were cancelled because of plagiarism and forgery in their postgraduate theses cannot reapply for associate professorship using these theses. The applications of candidates found to have applied in breach of this provision are rejected by the Inter-University Council Presidency.

Candidates whose associate professorship application has not been concluded cannot reapply for associate professorship in the same or a different scientific/artistic field.

## Is Obtaining the Associate Professorship Title the Same as Being Appointed to an Associate Professorship Post?

Obtaining the associate professorship title and appointment to an associate professorship post are different procedures. This is expressly regulated in Article 21, sub-paragraphs 3-d and e of the Higher Education Law no. 2547.

In this context, for appointment to an associate professor post, higher education institutions must first announce the post, and an application must be made to the announced associate professor post. In addition to holding the associate professorship title, higher education institutions may, with the approval of the Council of Higher Education, determine additional conditions of an objective and verifiable nature, solely for the purpose of improving scientific quality and taking into account the differences between scientific or artistic disciplines. Where an oral examination is among the additional conditions determined by higher education institutions, this examination is conducted by juries formed by the Inter-University Council.

In conclusion, we can list the appointment process, in order, as: announcement of the post – application – review of the application – decision of the university or institute of high technology board of directors and the appointment made by the Rector.

## Annulment Actions Against Acts Concerning the Rejection of an Associate Professorship Application

Against administrative acts concerning the rejection of an associate professorship application, one may object to the Inter-University Council, or apply directly to the courts against these acts within the time limit. All objections of candidates and jury members concerning the associate professorship process, and matters to be determined ex officio, are reviewed and decided by the Associate Professorship Commission.

## Competent Court

In annulment actions brought against administrative acts concerning the rejection of an associate professorship application, the court with subject-matter jurisdiction is the administrative courts, and the court with territorial jurisdiction is the administrative court at the place where the administrative authority that issued the administrative act or concluded the administrative contract subject to the action is located.
