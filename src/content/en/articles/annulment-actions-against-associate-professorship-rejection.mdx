## Who is an associate professor?

The definition is set out in Article 3(m)(2) of Higher Education Law No. 2547. Under it, an **associate professor** (doçent) is a person who holds the academic title of associate professor awarded by the Inter-University Council.

## What is the Inter-University Council (ÜAK)?

Under Articles 3(b) and 11 of Law No. 2547, the Inter-University Council is composed of the rectors of the universities and one faculty member periodically elected by each university senate.

## How applications are assessed

Associate professorship applications are assessed by juries formed within ÜAK. Jurors examine the candidate's work and issue a positive or negative report. Applications assessed negatively by the majority of the jury are rejected.

## Remedies against rejection

The ÜAK decision rejecting an application is an administrative act, so the following remedies are available:

### 1. Administrative objection

An objection may be made to the higher body (the ÜAK General Assembly) after notification of the rejection. This route is optional.

### 2. Annulment action

An annulment action can be filed with the Ankara Administrative Court within **60 days** of notification of the rejection. The period is strict and must be observed.

### 3. Request for stay of execution

A stay of execution can be requested together with the annulment action. If the court grants it, enforcement of the decision is suspended.

## What must be shown in the action

- Lack of objectivity in the jury assessment
- Procedural breaches (jury composition, process, notification)
- Incorrect calculation of publication criteria
- Errors in the ethics assessment

## Conclusion

Rejection decisions must be lawful. Procedural and substantive errors in the assessment can be challenged before the administrative court. If your application is rejected, we recommend consulting a lawyer without missing the 60-day deadline.
