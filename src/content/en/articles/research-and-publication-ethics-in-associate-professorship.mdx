## Research and publication ethics

Under Article 6 of the Associate Professorship Regulation, the application files of candidates are also examined by the assessment jury from the standpoint of research and publication ethics.

## Categories of ethical breach

The main categories of ethical breach assessed in the process are:

- **Plagiarism:** Using another person's ideas or work without citing the source
- **Falsification:** Manipulating or altering research data
- **Fabrication:** Creating data or findings that do not exist
- **Duplication:** Publishing the same work in more than one journal

## Legal process following an ethics allegation

If the jury issues a negative report on ethics grounds, the candidate has several options:

1. **Administrative objection:** Objection to the ÜAK General Assembly
2. **Annulment action:** Filed with the Ankara Administrative Court within 60 days
3. **Stay of execution:** May be requested together with the annulment action

## Points to watch when challenging an ethics-based rejection

Legal and technical errors in the jury report should be addressed separately:

- Whether the jurors work in the candidate's field
- Whether the plagiarism rate was calculated correctly
- Whether the difference between self-citation and plagiarism was taken into account
- Whether procedural rules were followed in the assessment

## Conclusion

Rejections on scientific ethics grounds are not always lawful. Errors in the jury report, procedural breaches and inconsistent assessment standards can be grounds for annulment before the administrative court.
