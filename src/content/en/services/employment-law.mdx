## Employment law for employees and employers

Turkish employment law governs every stage from formation to termination of the employment contract. Most disputes arise at termination and are time-sensitive: the periods for mediation, reinstatement and compensation claims are short.

We act for both employees and employers. On the employer side, the aim is to make termination, discipline and documentation processes compliant before a dispute arises.

## Employee claims and compensation

- Severance and notice pay
- Reinstatement (invalid termination) claims
- Overtime, weekly rest and public holiday pay
- Annual leave pay
- Just-cause resignation and compensation for workplace harassment
- Material and moral damages for work accidents and occupational diseases
- Compensation for breach of equal treatment

You can estimate amounts with the [severance and notice pay calculator](/en/calculators/severance-and-notice-pay). Actual figures depend on payroll and working conditions.

## Advice to employers

- Drafting employment contracts, workplace regulations and disciplinary procedures
- Planning terminations: taking the employee's defence, termination notice, release documents
- Setting up lawful performance assessment
- Representing employers in mediation
- Labour inspections and Social Security Institution (SGK) matters
- Protection of employee personal data

The reason for termination must be stated clearly and precisely. Because a different reason cannot be relied on later, it is worth getting legal input before the notice is written.

## Mandatory mediation

Mediation is mandatory before suing for employee and employer receivables, compensation and reinstatement. The mediator concludes the process within three weeks of the application; this may be extended by up to one week where necessary.

If the parties settle, the agreement signed by the parties and their lawyers is treated as a court judgment for enforcement. See [Mediation](/en/services/mediation) for details.

## Right after a dismissal

- Keep the termination notice and the date you received it.
- Gather payslips, bank records, correspondence and witness details.
- Have any release or settlement document reviewed before signing.
- If you seek reinstatement, do not miss the one-month mediation deadline.
