    a: "At foundation higher education institutions, tuition is set by the board of trustees. For years other than the preparatory and first year, the rate of increase is set by the Council of Higher Education (YÖK) and cannot exceed the average of the annual Domestic Producer Price Index and Consumer Price Index increases as of June. Fees are announced on the university’s website by the end of July each year, and an announced fee cannot be increased afterwards."
  - q: "Can a student object to a disciplinary penalty and file a lawsuit?"

A person directly harmed by the act constituting the disciplinary offence may object in the same way. The executive board decides on the objection within fifteen days. If the objection is accepted, the competent disciplinary authority or board decides again within thirty days, taking the grounds of acceptance into account. ## The Legal Structure of Foundation Universities

Foundation universities are higher education institutions with public legal personality, established by foundations by law on a non-profit basis. They consist of faculties, institutes, schools, vocational schools and similar units, and carry out teaching, scientific research, publishing and consultancy activities. They may spend their income only on developing their own university and the institutions and organisations affiliated with it.

Foundation universities are financially and administratively autonomous. Nevertheless, they remain subject to the supervision and oversight of the Council of Higher Education (YÖK) in matters of teaching and administrative, financial and economic affairs.

Public law and private law relationships coexist at foundation universities. This structure directly affects which court has jurisdiction in a dispute. Because a case filed in the wrong court can lead to loss of time and rights, identifying the correct forum at the first step is important.

From 2014 to 2020 Av. Kazım Onur Gerçek served as senior counsel at a foundation higher education institution. During that period he handled the institution’s judicial and administrative litigation, contract management, data protection (KVKK) compliance and its matters and transactions before YÖK.

## Student Disputes

- **Disciplinary penalties:** Preparing the defence and representation during investigations into cheating, plagiarism and other disciplinary matters; objections and annulment actions against warnings, reprimands, suspension and dismissal from the institution
- **Registration matters:** Objections and annulment actions concerning deregistration, termination of student status, registration freezes, expiry of the maximum study period, graduation, excuse and make-up exam rights
- **Exams, grades and grade point average:** Objections and annulment actions concerning exam grades, grade point average, transcript errors, and the assessment of projects, assignments, seminars and graduation theses
- **Fees and scholarships:** Objections and actions concerning tuition, preparatory school, summer school and single-course fees, scholarship status, and refunds after deregistration
- **Transfers and programmes:** Objections and annulment actions concerning rejected lateral and vertical transfer applications, exemption and course equivalency decisions, and admission to or removal from double major and minor programmes
- **Graduate studies:** Objections and annulment actions against decisions on applications to and admission into master’s, doctoral and proficiency in the arts programmes, failure in the doctoral qualifying exam, changes of jury and supervisor, and rejection of or required corrections after a thesis defence

## Academic and Administrative Staff Disputes

For disputes over pay and personnel rights involving academic and administrative staff at foundation higher education institutions, determining the correct forum is of great importance in preventing loss of time and rights.

### Academic staff

- **Disciplinary investigations:** Preparing the defence and representation during the investigation; objections and annulment actions against the disciplinary penalties listed in Article 53 of Law No. 2547
- **Publication ethics and plagiarism allegations:** Ethics committee reviews, objections to similarity reports, and objections and annulment actions against publication ethics violation decisions
- **Associate professorship, appointment and promotion:** [Rejection of an associate professorship application](/en/services/associate-professorship-annulment-actions); objections and annulment actions concerning appointment and promotion to professor, associate professor and assistant professor posts, jury reports, appointment criteria and announcement requirements
- **Termination of contracts:** Objections and actions against non-renewal or termination of academic staff contracts
- **Pay and working conditions:** Actions concerning pay, severance and notice compensation, annual leave, extra teaching hours, overtime, weekly rest days, and public holiday pay
- **Scholarships and compulsory service:** Actions concerning graduate scholarships, compulsory service obligations, and demands for repayment of scholarship amounts
- **Administrative posts:** Objections and actions concerning administrative duties such as dean and head of department
- **Academic mobbing:** Actions concerning systematic attacks on personality rights, forced resignation, and unjustified disciplinary investigations

### Administrative staff

- **Job security and reinstatement:** Reinstatement actions, compensation for failure to reinstate, and pay for the period of idleness
- **Employment receivables:** Actions concerning pay, severance and notice compensation, annual leave, overtime, weekly rest days, and public holiday pay, and the mandatory pre-litigation mediation process

## Advisory Services for Foundation Higher Education Institutions

Because they are subject to both higher education legislation and private law, foundation higher education institutions need regular legal support.

- **Internal regulations:** Drafting and updating regulations and directives; reviewing the compliance of Senate and board of trustees decisions with legislation
- **YÖK processes:** YÖK supervision and oversight, programme opening, quota and quality processes; support in matters and transactions before YÖK and ÜAK
- **Academic and administrative staff:** Personnel contracts, academic post announcements, appointment and promotion processes, renewal and termination of contracts
- **Student relations:** Registration and tuition contracts, compliance of fee refund policies with consumer legislation, scholarship directives
- **Disciplinary investigations:** Proper conduct of disciplinary investigations into students and staff, support for investigators, review of decisions for risk of annulment
- **Contracts:** Management of contracts relevant to the university, from drafting through follow-up, including procurement, maintenance and repair, educational cooperation and confidentiality contracts
- **Data protection compliance:** Preparing privacy notices, data inventories and policies; support with day-to-day data protection questions within the institution ([Legal Risk Management and Compliance](/en/services/legal-risk-management-and-compliance))
- **Litigation and dispute resolution:** Following administrative and judicial cases brought by students and staff, and representation in pre-litigation mediation
- **Training:** Training for managers and academic and administrative staff on disciplinary investigations, data protection and personnel management
- **University–industry cooperation:** R&D and project contracts, TÜBİTAK-supported projects, confidentiality and data sharing contracts; industrial property rights in inventions made at the university, licence and technology transfer contracts; technopark and technology transfer office processes, and establishment of academic spin-off companies
