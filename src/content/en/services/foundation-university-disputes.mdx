## Why are disputes at foundation universities different?

Foundation universities in Turkey are established by non-profit foundations, but their higher education activities are governed by Higher Education Law No. 2547 and supervised by the Council of Higher Education (YÖK). This dual structure means that a dispute at the same institution may belong before the labour court, the administrative court or the civil courts.

Identifying the correct forum at the outset is essential. A case filed in the wrong court costs time and often rights.

From 2014 to 2020 Av. Kazım Onur Gerçek served as senior counsel at a foundation university, handling the institution's litigation and contracts, its data protection compliance and its proceedings before YÖK.

## Student disputes

- Disciplinary investigations and annulment actions against disciplinary penalties
- Challenges to deregistration, termination of student status and diploma decisions
- Applications concerning exams, grades and assessment
- Scholarship, discount and tuition fee disputes
- Transfer, double major and leave of absence requests

In student disciplinary matters, courts focus on the right of defence, the impartiality of the investigator and the proportionality of the penalty. The written defence submitted during the investigation is often decisive in the later case.

## Academic staff disputes

At foundation universities, faculty members and lecturers work under employment contracts, so employment law and higher education legislation apply together.

- Termination or non-renewal of contracts
- Salary, additional teaching pay and benefit claims
- Position, title and promotion processes
- Disciplinary investigations
- Annulment actions against the rejection of associate professorship applications ([Associate Professorship Annulment Actions](/en/services/associate-professorship-annulment-actions))

Mediation is mandatory before suing for receivables and compensation under an employment contract. To challenge a dismissal, the application must be made within one month of the termination notice.

## Administrative staff disputes

- Termination, severance and notice pay
- Overtime and wage claims
- Workplace harassment (mobbing) claims
- Reassignment and material changes to working conditions

## Actions against YÖK and ÜAK decisions

Decisions of YÖK, the Inter-University Council (ÜAK) and university governing bodies are administrative acts. Annulment actions against them are generally filed with the administrative court within 60 days of notification. The time limit is strict, applied by the court of its own motion and cannot be extended.

If the administration does not answer an application within 60 days, this counts as an implied rejection, and the filing period starts when that silence period ends.

## Advice for institutions

Foundation universities themselves need regular legal support:

- Drafting regulations, directives and internal rules
- Contracts for academic and administrative staff
- Conducting disciplinary investigations properly
- Data protection compliance and protection of student data ([Legal Risk Management and Compliance](/en/services/legal-risk-management-and-compliance))
- Training for staff and managers
