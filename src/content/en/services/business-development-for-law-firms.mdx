## Scope of the service

Law school teaches law, not how to run a practice. As caseloads grow, deadline tracking, client communication, billing and team management take as much time as the legal work itself.

Av. Kazım Onur Gerçek worked in an institution's in-house legal department from 2014 to 2020 and has run his own practice since October 2020.

## Firm structure

- Comparing independent practice, joint practice and law partnership
- Putting the division of work and income between partners in writing
- Structure for associate lawyers, trainees and support staff
- Role descriptions and responsibilities

## Workflows

- A standard process from file opening to closing
- Deadline and hearing tracking
- Document templates and a pleadings library
- Client reporting routines
- Billing, expenses and receipts

Even in a small firm, a written file-opening checklist and a shared deadline calendar prevent the most common errors.

## Fee policy and engagement agreements

- Fee structures consistent with the minimum fee tariff
- Retainer agreements and fixed-fee models
- Engagement agreement template

## The advertising ban and online visibility

Lawyers may present their practice within the rules on the advertising ban. On websites, social media and directories, the line between information and advertising must be respected. Success figures, assertive slogans and offers below the fee tariff cross that line.

Informative content about your practice areas is the most solid way to gain visibility within these rules.

## Internal KVKK and technology rules

- Client privacy notice
- Rules for AI, email and cloud services
- Physical and digital file security
- Internal data breach procedure

See [KVKK Practice Guide for Lawyers](/en/articles/kvkk-guide-for-lawyers) for a detailed discussion.

## In-house legal teams

- Setting up or restructuring the legal function
- Working model and reporting with external counsel
- Contract approval processes and internal rules
