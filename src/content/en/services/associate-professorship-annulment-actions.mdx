faqs:
  - q: "When can I reapply if my associate professorship application is rejected?"
    a: "The time at which you may reapply depends on the reason for the rejection. Where an application is rejected for missing documents or non-compliance with procedure, you may reapply in the following periods; where it is cancelled for failure to meet the minimum requirements, you may reapply in the following period once the deficiencies are remedied. A candidate found unsuccessful by the jury, or whose application is cancelled for an ethics violation, may reapply at the earliest in the third following period. Unless ÜAK determines otherwise, applications are made twice a year, in March and October."
  - q: "Can the rejection of an associate professorship application be objected to?"
    a: "Yes. Candidates' objections concerning the associate professorship process are reviewed and decided by the Associate Professorship Commission. An objection is not mandatory; an annulment action may also be filed directly."
  - q: "In which court and where is an annulment action against the rejection of an associate professorship application filed?"
    a: "Annulment actions against administrative acts concerning the rejection of an associate professorship application are filed before the Ankara Administrative Courts, at the place where the Inter-University Council Presidency, which issued the administrative act, is located."
  - q: "Within what period is an annulment action against the rejection filed?"
    a: "The annulment action is filed within 60 days of notification of the decision to the candidate. The result of the application is made available in the Associate Professorship Information System and is deemed notified to the candidate on the fifth day following the date on which it was made available."
  - q: "Can I reapply for associate professorship while an annulment action against the rejection of my application is pending?"
    a: "Yes. The provision added to the Associate Professorship Regulation in 2021 that made the reapplication of a candidate who had sued against a rejection, cancellation or failure decision conditional on the case becoming final (Art. 4/6) was annulled by the Council of State 8th Chamber's decision of 26.05.2023, E. 2021/6349, K. 2023/2860. That decision became final with the affirming decision of the Council of State Plenary Session of Administrative Chambers dated 21.04.2025. Accordingly, while the case is pending, you may reapply once the waiting period provided for the type of decision has expired. The restriction that remains in force in the Regulation is that a candidate whose application has not yet been concluded cannot reapply; the criterion here is the status of the application, not of the case. Since the result of the new application may affect the case, it is advisable to plan the two processes together."
  - q: "Is obtaining the associate professorship title the same as being appointed to an associate professor post?"
    a: "No. The associate professorship title is a determination of the candidate's scientific competence and is awarded by the Inter-University Council. Appointment to an associate professor post is a separate process: an application is made to a post announced by the university, the university applies its own assessment and any additional conditions, and the university makes the appointment. Obtaining the title therefore does not automatically give a right to appointment to an associate professor post."

The provisions on the determination of associate professorship scientific/artistic fields, the conditions for applying, the time and procedure of application, the formation of associate professorship evaluation juries, the conduct of the associate professorship process, and the formation and duties of the commissions and the procedures to be followed for allegations of plagiarism, other breaches of scientific research and publication ethics, and disciplinary violations determined by the Inter-University Council, are carried out by the Inter-University Council (ÜAK) within the framework of the Higher Education Law no. 2547 and the Associate Professorship Regulation.

The rejection or cancellation of an associate professorship application, or a finding that the candidate is unsuccessful, is an administrative act; objection and administrative judicial remedies are available against these acts. Our firm provides the necessary legal support at every stage of the associate professorship process.

We provide legal support on the following matters, from objection proceedings before the Associate Professorship Commission to annulment actions in the administrative courts:

- **Rejection of the application:** Rejection decisions issued by the ÜAK Presidency on the ground of missing documents or non-compliance with the application procedure.
- **Minimum application requirements:** Decisions cancelling an application on the ground that the minimum requirements are not met; disputes over publication, citation and point calculations and over which period's criteria apply to the application.
- **Negative jury reports:** Decisions of failure based on jury reports that are not detailed and reasoned or that cannot be reviewed.
- **Ethics violation decisions:** Preparing the defence in proceedings before the Scientific Research and Publication Ethics Commission on allegations of plagiarism, fabrication, distortion, duplicate publication, salami slicing or unjust authorship; decisions cancelling an application on the ground of an ethics violation or of submitting misleading information or documents.
- **Disciplinary proceedings arising from ethics violation decisions:** Representation and preparation of the defence in disciplinary investigations that begin when the decision is notified to the higher education institution to which the candidate is affiliated ([Higher Education Law](/en/services/foundation-university-disputes)).
- **Appointment to an associate professor post:** Disputes arising from foundation universities' appointments to associate professor posts and from the additional conditions they set.
