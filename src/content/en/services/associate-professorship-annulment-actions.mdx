## The rejection is an administrative act

The title of associate professor (doçent) is awarded following an assessment run by the Inter-University Council (ÜAK). A rejection is an administrative act of ÜAK and is subject to review by the administrative courts.

Rejections are not always lawful. Insufficient reasoning in jury reports, application of the wrong criteria and procedural errors are the most common issues raised in annulment actions.

## When is an action filed?

- **Minimum requirements not met:** Whether publications, citations and points were calculated correctly is examined.
- **Negative jury reports:** Whether the reports contain concrete, reviewable reasoning and whether the jurors are qualified in the relevant field is assessed.
- **Publication ethics allegations:** In claims of plagiarism, fabrication or falsification, the process and evidence used to establish the allegation matter.
- **Changes to criteria:** When criteria change during the application process, which rule applies can be disputed.

## The process step by step

1. **Notification:** Time runs from notification of the rejection. Keep a record of the date.
2. **File review:** The jury reports and application file are obtained, and each ground for rejection is addressed.
3. **Filing:** The action is filed within 60 days of notification, as a rule with the Ankara Administrative Court.
4. **Stay of execution:** If enforcement of the decision would cause irreparable harm, a stay of execution can be requested.
5. **Expert examination:** In disputes over scientific assessment, the court frequently appoints experts.
6. **Judgment and after:** If the decision is annulled, ÜAK must reassess. Judgments can be appealed.

## Rejection on publication ethics grounds

An allegation of breach of research and publication ethics has the heaviest consequences, affecting not only the application but also the candidate's academic reputation.

In these files it should be examined whether the allegation was properly investigated by the relevant ethics committee, whether the candidate was given an opportunity to respond and how similarity rates were interpreted.

## First steps after a rejection

- Note the notification date; the 60-day period runs from it.
- Obtain the full jury reports and your application file.
- If you plan to apply in a new round, consider it together with the litigation strategy.
