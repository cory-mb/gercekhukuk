## What legal risk management means

A company's legal risks rarely start with a statement of claim. They start with a signed contract, a collected form or an unanswered request. Legal risk management means spotting these points in advance and setting rules that fit how the company actually works.

The aim is not to eliminate every risk, but to know which risks are being taken knowingly.

## Legal risk analysis

- Review of the contract portfolio: term, penalties, liability caps, jurisdiction and notice clauses
- Assessment of pending and potential litigation and enforcement files
- Audit of employment processes: contracts, termination and disciplinary practice
- Compliance with consumer, commercial electronic messaging and e-commerce rules
- Approval and authority matrix

## KVKK compliance programme

Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies to every business that processes personal data. Compliance is not a one-off document pack; it has to become part of daily operations.

- Data inventory and data flow map
- Determining whether VERBİS registration is required, and registration
- Privacy notices for employees, candidates, customers and visitors
- Identifying the correct legal ground; using explicit consent only where needed
- Retention and destruction policy
- Contracts with data processors
- Internal process for answering data subject requests within 30 days
- Staff training

In compliance work, what usually takes longest is not writing the documents but getting departments used to new rules, so the work ends with short, workable internal rules.

## Data breaches and Board procedures

- Breach response plan and 72-hour notification procedure
- Preparing the breach notification to the Board
- Representation in Board complaints and investigations
- Remedies against administrative fines

For breach of data security obligations, the administrative fine range applicable in 2026 is TRY 256,357 to TRY 17,092,242.

## Transfers abroad and AI tools

Email, cloud storage, booking systems and generative AI tools often run on servers abroad. Uploading personal data to them may amount to a data transfer. The tools in use should be listed, the necessary safeguards put in place and written rules adopted for staff.

For law firms specifically, see our article [KVKK Practice Guide for Lawyers](/en/articles/kvkk-guide-for-lawyers).

## Internal policies and training

- Company regulations, procedures and policies
- Information security and acceptable use rules
- Training for managers and staff
