## Legal support in family matters

Family disputes are as personal as they are legal. A divorce determines not only financial matters such as alimony and property, but also the children's future. Running the process as predictably as possible is therefore important.

Family cases are heard by the family courts and governed by Turkish Civil Code No. 4721.

## Divorce

### Uncontested divorce

In marriages of at least one year, if the spouses agree on the divorce and its consequences, the case often concludes in a single hearing. The protocol is decisive: alimony, custody, contact and property should be written clearly and enforceably. A vague clause becomes the source of new cases after the divorce.

### Contested divorce

- Fundamental breakdown of the marriage
- Adultery
- Attempt on life, severe maltreatment or degrading conduct
- Crime and dishonourable life
- Desertion
- Mental illness
- De facto separation

Some specific grounds are subject to short deadlines. A case for adultery or attempt on life must be filed within six months of learning of the ground.

## Alimony

- **Interim alimony:** For the spouse and children during the case.
- **Child support:** The non-custodial parent's contribution to the child's expenses.
- **Poverty alimony:** For a spouse who would fall into poverty because of the divorce, provided their fault is not greater.

A separate case can be filed to increase, reduce or end alimony based on changes in the parties' financial situation.

## Custody and contact

The decisive criterion is the child's best interests. Custody can be reassessed if circumstances change. Arranging contact and remedies where contact is not respected also fall under this heading.

## Division of marital property

For property acquired after 1 January 2002, the default regime is participation in acquired property. The value of assets acquired during the marriage is divided between the spouses under statutory rules. Personal property and inheritances are excluded.

- Participation and value-increase claims
- Family home annotation
- Marital property agreements

## Protective orders

In cases of domestic violence, protective measures such as removal orders can be requested under Law No. 6284. These requests are handled urgently and independently of any divorce case.
