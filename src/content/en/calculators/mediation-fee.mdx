## How it is calculated

Mediation fees follow the Mediation Minimum Fee Tariff published each year by the Ministry of Justice. This calculator uses the 2026 tariff published in the Official Gazette of 26 December 2025 and assumes a single mediator.

**On settlement:** A proportional fee is calculated on the settlement amount with tiered rates. If it is lower than the minimum fee for the dispute type, the minimum applies.

**Without settlement:** In mandatory mediation, two hours of the hourly fee set for the dispute type and number of parties are paid.

## Reading the result

The tariff sets minimum amounts; the parties may agree a higher fee with the mediator. Rates differ where more than one mediator acts.

For process, deadlines and the effect of the settlement agreement, see [Mediation](/en/services/mediation).
