## How it is calculated

New rent = current rent × (1 + increase rate). Under Article 344 of the Turkish Code of Obligations, the increase may not exceed the change in the 12-month average CPI.

The Turkish Statistical Institute (TÜİK) publishes this figure on the 3rd of each month together with the previous month's inflation data. For contracts renewed in October 2026, the rate is **31.49%**, based on the September data published on 5 October 2026.

If your contract renews in another month, enter the latest rate published at that date in the rate field.

## Can the parties agree on a lower rate?

Yes. The legal rate is a ceiling. The parties may agree on a lower increase or none at all.

## If no agreement is reached

The landlord may file a rent determination action, after first applying to mediation. For rent determination, eviction and rent claims, see [Law of Obligations](/en/services/law-of-obligations).
