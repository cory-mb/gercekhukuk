## How it is calculated

Severance pay equals 30 days' gross wages for each full year of service. Remaining periods are counted pro rata. This calculator divides the total days of service by 365.

The amount for each year may not exceed the **severance ceiling** in force when the contract ends. The 2026 ceilings are:

## Who is entitled to severance pay?

The employee must have worked for the same employer for at least one year, and the contract must end for a reason listed in the law, such as termination by the employer without just cause, termination by the employee for just cause, retirement, military service or resignation by a female employee within one year of marriage.

## Notice pay

A party terminating an indefinite-term contract without observing the notice period must pay wages for that period. The period ranges from two to eight weeks depending on length of service.

## Reading the result

The result is an estimate based on your inputs. Overtime, bonuses, benefits, amounts actually paid and the reason for termination can change it. Income tax on notice pay may fall in a different bracket depending on your total income for the year.
