## How it is calculated

Statutory interest is simple interest: principal × annual rate × days ÷ 365. If the rate changed during the period, the period is split and each part is calculated at its own rate.

## The 2026 change

Law No. 7589, published in the Official Gazette of 31 July 2026, amended Article 1 of Law No. 3095 on Statutory and Default Interest. The rate is no longer fixed; it is set each year by reference to the Central Bank rediscount rate. If the rediscount rate changes by five points or more by mid-year, the rate is reset for the second half of the year.

## Reading the result

Interest runs from the date the debtor is in default, usually established by a formal notice. If the contract sets an interest rate, that rate applies. For collecting a claim through enforcement, see [Enforcement Law](/en/services/enforcement-law).
